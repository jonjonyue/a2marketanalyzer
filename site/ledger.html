    How this is calculated

    • Sold: you receive price × quantity, minus the sales tax (default 10%) and the listing fee (default 2%). Enter the price you listed it at; the deductions are worked out per entry and rounded to the nearest kinah.
    • Bought: you pay price × quantity. The buyer pays no tax on the exchange.
    • Materials (first in the item list) is a catch-all for anything you bought to craft with. Log it as Bought, either as one entry with quantity 1 and the total you spent, or per material. It counts against your profit like any purchase, and shows as "Crafting materials" in the by-item table.
    • Listed: posting a listing costs the listing fee (default 2% of price × quantity) right away, whether or not it sells. It then shows under Active listings, where you can mark some or all of it sold (each sale deducts only the sales tax, since the fee was already paid), mark it expired (the fee is lost), or relist the unsold units (a new listing with a new fee). Listings expire after 3 days. Use Sold directly for sales you didn't log as a listing; those deduct both tax and fee.
    • Profit = everything received from sales − everything spent buying − listing fees paid, within the selected period. Items you bought but haven't sold yet count against you until you sell them; "On hand" shows how many are still unsold.
    • Prices accept shorthand: 250k, 1.5m, 2b, or plain numbers with commas.
