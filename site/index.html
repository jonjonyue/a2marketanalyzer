How this is calculated

  • Cost per craft comes from aion2.exchange's crafting model: expected value over many crafts at mastery 100, with each material priced at the cheapest of buying it, converting Odyle, or crafting it from cheaper inputs. "Buy everything" uses the site's all-market cost instead.
  • Sell price: "Conservative" takes the lower of the cheapest current listing and the 28-day average sale, so a single overpriced listing can't inflate the margin.
  • Profit per craft = sell price × (1 − exchange fee) − cost. Set the fee to whatever the exchange charges.
  • Sold per day = units sold in the last 28 days ÷ 28, from the game's own trade statistics. Days of supply = current listings ÷ sold per day.
  • Daily profit pool = profit per craft × sold per day; your daily scales that by the share of sales you expect to win. Crafts are ranked by the pool.
  • Verdicts: Craft it = profitable, at least 0.5 sold/day, and under 14 days of supply. Slow seller and Oversupplied are profitable on paper but hard to move.
